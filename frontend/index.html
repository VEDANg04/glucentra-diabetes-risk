About the model

Predict risk without medical data.

Glucentra is trained on the BRFSS 2015 dataset — a CDC survey of 250,000+ Americans. It uses only lifestyle and demographic information, so anyone can assess their risk without a prior clinic visit.

Hereditary risk is layered on top using relative-risk multipliers from published epidemiological literature, so family history is properly accounted for even though it wasn't in the original dataset.
